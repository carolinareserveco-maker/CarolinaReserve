Carolina Reserve Classica

Ecuadorian Connecticut Shade • Indonesian Sumatra • Dominican Seco • Brazilian Mata Fina

A creamy, approachable cigar with honey sweetness, floral lift, soft spice, and a smooth finish. The Connecticut Shade wrapper keeps the smoke elegant while letting the filler blend show through.

  • Wrapper: Ecuadorian Connecticut Shade
  • Binder: Indonesian Sumatra
  • Filler: Dominican Seco and Brazilian Mata Fina
  • Body: Mild-Medium
  • Profile: Honey, cream, floral notes, smooth tobacco

About the Claro

Soft, creamy, floral, and smooth without being boring.

Carolina Reserve Classica is designed as the lighter side of the house: polished, creamy, aromatic, and easy to enjoy. The Ecuadorian Connecticut Shade wrapper brings honey, cream, and floral notes while the Indonesian Sumatra binder and Dominican/Brazilian filler create depth beneath the smooth finish.
