The Maduro Collection

Signature Broadleaf Blends

Three handmade-style blends using Connecticut Broadleaf wrapper and Dominican/Nicaraguan filler profiles.

01

Sweet Cream Reserve

70% Dominican Piloto Cubano • 30% Nicaraguan Jalapa

The smoothest blend in the lineup. Cream-forward with soft chocolate, light coffee, vanilla-like sweetness, and a mellow finish.

  • Wrapper: Connecticut Broadleaf
  • Body: Mild-Medium
  • Profile: Cream, milk chocolate, caramel
02

Carolina Reserve Mocha

60% Dominican Piloto Cubano • 40% Nicaraguan Jalapa

The flagship blend. Smooth, creamy, and naturally sweet with notes of milk chocolate, café mocha, sweet cream, and soft roasted coffee.

  • Wrapper: Connecticut Broadleaf
  • Body: Medium
  • Profile: Chocolate, cream, coffee
03

Velours Luxe Maduro

50% Dominican Piloto Cubano • 50% Nicaraguan Jalapa

A richer, darker expression with deeper cocoa and espresso character while keeping a smooth, creamy finish. Built for smokers who want dessert without fake flavoring.

  • Wrapper: Connecticut Broadleaf
  • Body: Medium+
  • Profile: Dark chocolate, espresso, sweet tobacco

About the Maduro

A smooth maduro cigar concept from the Carolinas.

Carolina Reserve Maduros focus on balanced blends that avoid harsh pepper and heavy earth. Each cigar concept is designed around Connecticut Broadleaf sweetness, Dominican smoothness, and Nicaraguan Jalapa richness.
